# JooEvents licensing

JooEvents is source available. The complete product may be inspected, studied,
modified, and forked, and current versions are free for individuals, community
use, and smaller organizations under the [license](../../LICENSE).

## Why start here?

I am starting with these terms to keep my options open while JooEvents is young.
I want the software to be useful to individuals, community organizers, and
smaller organizations without putting a tollbooth in their way. At the same
time, I do not yet want to give up every option for asking larger commercial
users—or businesses that resell JooEvents itself—to contribute a reasonable
amount.

I will be considering Apache License 2.0 as JooEvents matures. That is a real
possibility, not a promise with a predetermined date. If a version is released
under Apache 2.0 in the future, the rights granted for that version will be
permanent.

## What is free?

The license permits, without asking:

- personal, educational, research, volunteer, and community use by individuals;
- production use by an organization with both fewer than 50 workers and less
  than US$5 million in consolidated annual revenue or operating budget;
- running that organization's own free or paid events, including access for
  applicants, speakers, reviewers, attendees, sponsors, volunteers, and staff;
- evaluation, development, testing, security research, and demonstrations by
  anyone;
- public forks and redistribution without charge; and
- paid consulting, installation, customization, training, and support for a
  customer that already has the right to use its deployment.

The thresholds apply to the whole organization and its controlled affiliates,
not merely the team, event, or subsidiary operating JooEvents.

Here is the candid version: I am trying to be reasonable, not set a licensing
trap. If arranging a commercial license before using JooEvents for your own
organization or events is inconvenient, you may use it first. You do not need
to chase me or wait for paperwork.

You need to act only if I send your organization a valid Commercial License
Notice from [bowlboy@joocorp.com](mailto:bowlboy@joocorp.com). You then have
180 days to work out a separate license with me or stop production use. Use
before the notice and during those 180 days is expressly authorized: there are
no retroactive fees or damages, and stopping by the deadline settles the
matter. If I never send that notice, the courtesy permission continues.

This courtesy applies to an organization's own operations and events. Please
contact me before:

- offering hosted or managed access to substantial JooEvents functionality;
- selling, white-labelling, or reselling JooEvents or a fork; or
- embedding substantial JooEvents functionality in a commercial product or
  service offered to others.

A reseller cannot avoid this requirement by starting from a community fork.
The conditions follow the covered JooEvents code.

## Intended commercial approach

Commercial licensing is intended to stay reasonable and low-friction:

- larger organizations using JooEvents internally can begin under the courtesy
  grant and, if contacted, should be able to obtain a fixed-fee, long-term
  license rather than negotiate an enterprise subscription;
- ordinary consultants should not owe royalties merely for helping a properly
  licensed customer;
- hosting, OEM, white-label, and resale businesses may use a fixed fee,
  per-customer pricing, or a reasonable share of revenue attributable to
  JooEvents; and
- a commercial license does not include support, hosting, service levels,
  warranties, or a promise of continued development unless a separate agreement
  expressly says so.

Commercial pricing is not publicly disclosed for now. You are welcome to
discuss a larger-organization license at any time; hosting, OEM, white-label,
and resale uses must be discussed before they begin. Contact
[bowlboy@joocorp.com](mailto:bowlboy@joocorp.com). Permission exists only when
a written commercial license has been agreed, except for the permissions the
public license already grants—including the larger-organization courtesy
grant. This page is a summary, not an additional license grant.

## Forks and branding

Forks are welcome, but a materially modified version must use its own primary
name and branding. It may truthfully say that it is based on or derived from
JooEvents. See the [trademark policy](../../TRADEMARKS.md) for the distinction
between copyright permission and permission to present something as official.

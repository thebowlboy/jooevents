# Security policy

JooEvents is pre-release software and is not yet ready for production event data.
Security reports are welcome regardless, and they are read.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **bowlboy@joocorp.com** with the details. Please include:

- what the issue is and the impact you believe it has;
- the steps, request, or input needed to reproduce it;
- the version, commit, or deployment you tested; and
- how you would like to be credited, if you would like to be.

Please report privately by email first rather than opening a public issue, so a
fix can ship before the details are public.

## What to expect

- An acknowledgement that the report arrived.
- An assessment of whether it reproduces, and what the impact is.
- A fix in a released version, and credit in the release notes if you want it.

JooEvents is maintained by one person and makes no service-level commitment. A
report will be handled as promptly as that allows.

## Scope

In scope: the JooEvents application, its published surfaces and embeds, its MCP
interface, and its authentication and authorization behavior.

Out of scope: findings in third-party services JooEvents integrates with, which
should go to those services; and reports produced only by denial-of-service or
automated-scanner output without a demonstrated issue.

## Safe harbour

Testing against your own installation or a deployment you are authorized to test
is welcome, and the license expressly permits security research. Do not test
against another organization's JooEvents deployment without their permission, and
do not access, modify, or retain data that is not yours.

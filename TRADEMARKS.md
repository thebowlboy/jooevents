# JooEvents trademark policy

Effective: 10 August 2026

JooEvents is open source, and its copyright license deliberately permits broad
reuse and modification. Trademark rights serve a narrower purpose: helping
people distinguish official JooEvents releases from other products and forks.

This policy describes uses that the owner of the JooEvents marks, publishing
under the pseudonym Bowlboy, permits without separate written approval. It does
not limit fair use, fair dealing, nominative use, or any other use that
applicable law permits.

## Covered marks

This policy covers the following marks, whether registered or unregistered:

- the name **JooEvents**;
- the JooEvents wordmark and official logos; and
- the name **Bowlboy**, the Bowlboy character, and official Bowlboy depictions
  when they are used to identify JooEvents or an official JooEvents release.

The Apache License 2.0 grants copyright permission to copy and adapt the
first-party artwork included in this repository. As stated in section 6 of that
license, it does not grant trademark permission. Artistic reuse is welcome; a
use that identifies the source of a product or service must also follow this
policy.

## Guiding rule

Your use must not mislead people about what they are receiving, who made it, or
whether Bowlboy or the JooEvents project sponsors, approves, maintains, or
endorses it.

## Uses allowed without additional permission

You may:

- redistribute an official, unmodified JooEvents release with its existing
  names and artwork;
- distribute a bit-for-bit build made from an unmodified official release if
  you identify who built it;
- truthfully describe software as "based on JooEvents," "derived from
  JooEvents," "compatible with JooEvents," or "for JooEvents";
- use the marks in reviews, tutorials, presentations, screenshots, commentary,
  and links to the project;
- use the marks for a clearly unofficial community group, site, or event when
  your own identity is more prominent and you state that it is independent;
  and
- create, share, or sell non-confusing Bowlboy artwork and remixes, provided
  that you credit the original character and artwork to Bowlboy/JooEvents,
  identify your modifications, and do not present them as official JooEvents
  branding or imply endorsement.

## Modified versions and forks

You may modify and distribute JooEvents under the Apache License 2.0. A
substantively modified version must use a distinct primary product name and
branding, clearly identify itself as modified and unofficial, and avoid using
the official JooEvents wordmark or official Bowlboy depiction as its own source
identifier. It may still make a truthful statement such as "derived from
JooEvents."

A suitable independence statement is:

> This is an independent project derived from JooEvents. It is not affiliated
> with or endorsed by Bowlboy or the JooEvents project.

## Uses requiring permission

Unless applicable law independently permits the use, ask first before:

- using a covered mark in a company, product, service, package, or domain name;
- presenting modified software primarily under the JooEvents name;
- using a mark for certification, official support, sponsorship, or endorsement;
  or
- using an official logo or Bowlboy depiction in a way likely to make a product,
  service, event, or publication appear official.

Request permission through the canonical JooEvents repository. Permission for
one use does not imply permission for another.
